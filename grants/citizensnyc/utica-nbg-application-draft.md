# Utica Security Services Neighborhood Business Grant Draft

## In at least 150 words provide a detailed project description. Tell us what your project is. Explain what is going to be done beyond what you do in your everyday business operations.

Utica Security Services is proposing a Neighborhood Safety Preparedness Initiative designed to support local residents, small businesses, community groups, and event organizers through free and low-cost public safety education. While our everyday business operations include security services, consulting, fingerprinting, notary services, testing center operations, defensive driving, and NYS security guard training, this grant project would go beyond our normal paid services by making safety knowledge and preparedness resources directly accessible to the wider community.

Through this initiative, we will host a series of community-based workshops focused on practical safety topics such as situational awareness, emergency preparedness, de-escalation, event safety planning, and basic prevention strategies for neighborhood businesses and public-facing organizations. We also plan to offer a limited number of community safety assessments for small businesses and local organizations that may not otherwise be able to afford this kind of support. In addition, we will create printed and digital safety guidance that participants can use after each session.

This project is rooted in the belief that public safety should not only be available to paying clients. As a neighborhood-based business with direct experience in training, consulting, and security operations, Utica Security Services is well positioned to turn its professional expertise into a practical community benefit project that improves preparedness, strengthens local relationships, and helps neighbors feel more informed and supported.

## In at least 150 words why is the project needed in your community? What does a successful project look like?

This project is needed because many neighborhood businesses, residents, and community groups face day-to-day safety concerns but do not have affordable access to practical guidance on prevention, preparedness, and response. Small businesses are often expected to handle difficult situations involving customer conflict, neighborhood disorder, emergency planning, or event coordination without formal support. Residents and local organizers also benefit from clear, trusted safety education that is grounded in real-world experience rather than theory.

Utica Security Services sees firsthand how often people need help understanding how to prevent problems before they escalate. Our services already place us in contact with people seeking structured, professional support, whether through training, fingerprinting, testing, or security-related services. That experience gives us a direct view into the kinds of safety concerns that affect everyday neighborhood life. This project responds to that need by offering practical, locally accessible support in a format that people can actually use.

A successful project would look like a measurable increase in neighborhood preparedness and confidence. Success would include strong participation from local businesses and residents, completion of workshops and safety assessments, and clear evidence that participants walked away with tools they could apply immediately. Success would also mean stronger relationships between our business and the surrounding community, with participants seeing Utica Security Services not only as a service provider, but as a trusted neighborhood resource invested in public well-being.

## In at least 150 words how will your business use this project to strengthen relationships among community members and project participants? How will your business conduct outreach to the community in support of your project?

This project will strengthen relationships by creating opportunities for local people to learn, prepare, and problem-solve together around a shared community concern: safety. Rather than limiting our role to private client services, this initiative will invite residents, business owners, workers, and community organizers into a more collaborative relationship with our business and with one another. Workshops and safety sessions will create a space where participants can ask questions, exchange experiences, and build practical connections around neighborhood improvement.

Because Utica Security Services already offers public-facing services such as fingerprinting, notary support, testing center access, defensive driving, and security training, our business location is already a point of contact for many people in the community. We will use that visibility to promote the project through in-office flyers, direct outreach to existing local contacts, printed materials, social media, and word-of-mouth promotion. We will also seek to connect with local businesses, event organizers, and community-serving institutions that may benefit from participating or helping spread awareness.

Our goal is not only to teach safety practices, but to build trust. By making our expertise more accessible and community-centered, we can help participants feel supported rather than isolated in dealing with safety concerns. That relationship-building effect is an important part of the project’s impact, because stronger neighborhood connections make communities more informed, more confident, and more resilient.

## In at least 150 words how will the project be sustained after the grant period?

This project will be sustained after the grant period by building on the relationships, materials, and community trust established during the funded phase. The grant will help Utica Security Services launch and organize the initial programming, but the long-term value of the project comes from creating a repeatable model for neighborhood safety education and outreach. Once workshops, handouts, outreach materials, and community contacts are developed, they can continue to be used and adapted beyond the original grant period.

Because public safety education aligns closely with our existing expertise in training, consulting, and community-facing services, we are well positioned to continue offering selected workshops, preparedness guidance, and safety support in a lower-cost format after the grant ends. The project also creates an opportunity to build ongoing relationships with local businesses, residents, and community groups that may lead to future partnerships, referrals, volunteer support, or in-kind collaboration.

Sustainability also comes from becoming known as a neighborhood resource, not only a business vendor. As more people interact with Utica Security Services through this initiative, we expect the project to create a stronger foundation of trust and visibility that can support future programming. In that way, the grant would not fund a one-time event, but help establish a continuing community safety effort with lasting neighborhood benefit.
