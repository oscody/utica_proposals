# NBGSampleApplication2025


Neighborhood Business Grant Sample Application:

Narrative Response Examples


In at least 150 words provide a detailed project description. Tell us what your project
is. Explain what is going to be done beyond what you do in your everyday business
operations.

Since LES Fitness empowers formerly incarcerated individuals through fitness and
employment opportunities, our core project involves visits to facilities like Rikers Island
and juvenile detention centers. There we will teach incarcerated individuals the skills
needed to become certified group fitness instructors. By providing professional training
that opens career pathways upon release, we aim to transform lives through fitness, while
simultaneously promoting physical and mental well-being for those facing the challenges
of reentry. At LES Fitness, we believe that everyone deserves a second chance, and we are
dedicated to creating pathways for sustainable success.
Beyond providing employment opportunities, LES Fitness wants to support formerly
incarcerated people by connecting them with a community once their sentences are
complete. We are firm believers that the best way to feel connected to the community you
reside or work in is to get involved in neighborhood efforts to impact it in a positive way. We
want people connected with LES Fitness to give back and be exposed to the various ways
that they can support others. In our neighborhood in the Lower East side, we plan to
organize various drives that will give away coats, turkeys, and toys, which will connect our
LES Fitness workers and clients with people who are in need. We also want to organize
community cleanups in order to inspire our workers and clients to be better invested in
their blocks and their cleanliness. We hope through these efforts that we can create a
collaborative environment between LES Fitness and the Lower East Side.
Together, these two aspects of our project will create an example for how to meaningfully
support those who are incarcerated and their transition back to their communities. 




While we provide services to them, we also show them that they have value to provide to others.
In at least 150 words why is the project needed in your community? 

What does a successful project look like?

LES Fitness serves a crucial purpose in society as it directly addresses the significant
challenges faced by formerly incarcerated individuals when reentering the community.
These individuals often struggle with barriers such as limited job opportunities due to their
criminal record or lack of employable skills, leading to financial instability and a cycle of
hopelessness that can result in recidivism.
At LES Fitness, we aim to break this cycle by providing training and certification in group
fitness instruction, creating employment pathways for our participants, and fostering a
sense of community within our program. We strongly believe that having a steady job with
a living wage and a supportive network is vital for long-term success after incarceration.
By empowering individuals through fitness and professional development, we are not only
reducing recidivism rates but also supporting positive reintegration into society. We
understand the importance of a second chance and strive to provide our participants with
the necessary tools and support to help them succeed in their journey towards a better
future. By offering opportunities for growth and personal development, LES Fitness is
making a positive impact on the lives of formerly incarcerated individuals and promoting a
more inclusive community.

In at least 150 words how will your business use this project to strengthen relationships among community members and project participants? 

How will your business conduct outreach to the community in support of your project ?

Providing job training and connection to community to formerly incarcerated individuals is
crucial for their reintegration and benefits the neighborhoods they return to. After leaving
prison, many face feelings of isolation due to their past. A supportive community bridges
the gap by offering acceptance and understanding, fostering emotional stability and
personal growth. With some guidance, access to resources, and mentorship, individuals
are more likely to engage positively, reducing the chances of recidivism. By helping them
rebuild their lives, we strengthen our neighborhoods as they become active, productive
members who contribute to the community’s social and economic fabric. LES Fitness’
success is not just measured by the number of individuals we help secure employment for,
but also by the strong, supportive network we create. Our goal is to empower individuals to
break free from the cycle of incarceration and serve as role models for others facing similar
challenges, ultimately enhancing the overall well-being of our communities.


In at least 150 words how will the project be sustained after the grant period?

Our work will be sustained beyond the grant period through the strong community we have
built at LES Fitness. We will continue to foster relationships within prisons and at our
facility, encouraging formerly incarcerated individuals to form bonds with others who
share similar experiences. This network of support will empower participants to give back
and uplift their communities, creating a ripple effect that disrupts the cycle of recidivism
and promotes successful reintegration. Through strong partnerships with organizations
and businesses, we will provide ongoing job opportunities and resources to ensure their
continued success long after they leave LES Fitness. We will also advocate for criminal
justice reform and raise awareness about the barriers faced by returning citizens to drive
positive change in our communities. With determination and a commitment to lasting
impact, we believe LES Fitness can positively transform the lives of those who have been
marginalized and help them rebuild a brighter future.
