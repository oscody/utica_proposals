## Utica Security Services: How to Use Our Existing Services as Leverage for the CitizensNYC Neighborhood Business Grant

The grant is not for general business operations. It is for a specific community initiative that improves neighborhood life. That means Utica Security Services should position its current services as proof that it already has the trust, experience, location, and technical ability to run a meaningful public safety project in Queens.

## What We Already Offer

Utica Security Services is more than a private security company. Our business already provides community-facing services that residents and small businesses use directly, including:

- Event security
- Guard services
- Personal security
- Executive protection
- Commercial security
- Security consulting
- NYS security guard training
- NYS defensive driving course
- Fingerprinting
- Notary public
- Pearson VUE-authorized testing center services
- IdentoGO fingerprinting and enrollment services

These services matter because they show that our business is already rooted in the neighborhood, already trusted to handle safety-related responsibilities, and already equipped to organize training, coordinate people, and deliver structured public-facing services.

## How to Turn These Services Into a Winning Grant Case

CitizensNYC is looking for four things: need, relevance, urgency, and quality-of-life impact. Our application should use our services to support each one.

### 1. Need

We should show that many small businesses, faith institutions, event organizers, parents, and residents in Southeast Queens face real safety concerns but do not have affordable access to practical safety education, prevention planning, and preparedness support.

Strong framing:

"Many neighborhood businesses and residents experience safety concerns, but lack access to trusted, practical, and locally available guidance on prevention, preparedness, and safe community engagement. As a result, many operate without basic safety planning, de-escalation knowledge, or emergency readiness."

### 2. Relevance and Urgency

This is where our existing services become leverage.

We are relevant because:

- We already work in public safety
- We already train people
- We already operate in Queens
- We already interact with local residents and businesses through fingerprinting, testing, training, and security services
- We understand real on-the-ground safety concerns affecting businesses, events, and community spaces

Strong framing:

"Utica Security Services is uniquely positioned to lead this project because public safety is our core business, and our Jamaica, Queens location already serves residents, workers, and business owners through training, fingerprinting, testing, and security-related services. We are not entering the neighborhood as outsiders. We are building on trust, visibility, and operational experience we already have."

### 3. Quality of Life Impact

The application should make it clear that the grant will help us deliver a project that benefits the public, not paying clients.

Good outcomes to emphasize:

- Safer neighborhood events
- Better prepared small businesses
- More informed residents
- Improved emergency awareness
- Stronger relationships between local businesses and the people they serve
- Reduced fear and uncertainty around public safety

Strong framing:

"The project will improve quality of life by helping residents and business owners feel more informed, prepared, and connected around neighborhood safety. Beyond reducing risk, the initiative will strengthen trust and participation by creating opportunities for local people to learn together and support one another."

### 4. Why Now

We should explain why this work is urgent now, not just generally useful.

Examples:

- Small businesses continue to face safety concerns that affect operations, customer confidence, and community participation
- Community events need better low-cost safety support and planning
- Residents benefit from practical safety education that is local and accessible

Strong framing:

"This project is timely because neighborhood businesses and residents continue to face day-to-day safety concerns, yet many lack access to affordable, practical support. A small grant can help close that gap by making prevention-oriented safety education and preparedness resources available at the community level."

## Best Grant Project Angle

The strongest approach is to use our existing services as the foundation for a public-facing initiative, not to ask for funding for the services themselves.

Recommended concept:

## Neighborhood Safety Preparedness Initiative

A Queens-based community safety project led by Utica Security Services that offers free or low-cost workshops, business safety assessments, and neighborhood event preparedness support for local small businesses, community groups, and residents.

This works because it directly connects our expertise to public benefit.

## How Each Existing Service Helps the Case

### NYS Security Guard Training

This shows we already know how to teach structured safety content, manage attendance, and provide practical instruction. It supports credibility for workshops and educational programming.

### Security Consulting

This shows we can assess risks, identify vulnerabilities, and provide site-specific recommendations. It supports a project that includes neighborhood business safety assessments.

### Event Security

This shows we understand crowd safety, coordination, emergency awareness, and public-facing operations. It supports proposals focused on safer community gatherings and local events.

### Guard Services / Commercial Security / Personal Security

These show direct field experience with real-world safety issues. This helps prove that our project is informed by practical experience, not theory.

### Fingerprinting / IdentoGO / Pearson VUE / Notary

These services are especially useful in grant positioning because they show that our location is already a community access point. People already come to us for structured, trusted, administrative, and professional services. That makes it easier to argue that we are a neighborhood anchor business, not just a contractor.

### Defensive Driving

This helps broaden the community safety angle. It shows we support prevention, education, and safer day-to-day behavior, not just reactive security work.

## What To Avoid

The application will be weaker if we:

- Ask for funding for ordinary payroll, rent, or routine business expenses
- Describe client security work as community impact
- Sound too commercial or sales-oriented
- Focus more on our company growth than neighborhood benefit
- Fail to identify who benefits and how many people we expect to reach

## Strong Positioning Language

Use language like this throughout the application:

"Utica Security Services is a Jamaica, Queens-based small business that combines public safety expertise with trusted, community-facing services. Because we already provide training, fingerprinting, testing, consulting, and security support from a neighborhood-based location, we are well positioned to lead a practical community safety initiative that strengthens preparedness, trust, and quality of life for local residents and small businesses."

## Bottom Line

Our leverage is not simply that we provide security services. Our leverage is that:

- We are already rooted in Queens
- We already provide trusted public-facing services
- We already train people
- We already understand neighborhood safety issues
- We can turn that operational experience into a community benefit project with measurable impact
