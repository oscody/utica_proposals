Here are several grant and funding opportunities that fit the profile for Utica Security Services Inc., completely excluding 501(c)(3) restricted options and your previously reviewed list:

**1\. New York State Seed Funding Grant Program**

* **Funder:** New York State Empire State Development (ESD)  
* **Eligibility:** For-profit micro and small businesses incorporated in NY that started operations on or after September 1, 2018 (Utica Security launched in April 2021).

* **Funding Amount:** Up to $25,000, calculated mathematically based on 10% of gross receipts.

* **Deadline:** Ongoing / Rolling.

* **Application URL:** [https://nyseedgrant.com/](https://nyseedgrant.com/)

* **Rationale:** Provides immediate, unrestricted working capital that Utica Security can utilize for general operational expenses, payroll stabilization, or equipment upgrades.

**2\. Queens Small Business Grant Program**

* **Funder:** Queens Borough President, Pursuit, and local partners (RDRC/GJDC)  
* **Eligibility:** For-profit small businesses with 20 or fewer employees physically located within the Queens borough.  
* **Funding Amount:** Up to $20,000.

* **Deadline:** Rolling (Subject to fund availability).

* **Application URL:** [tinyurl.com/GJDCGrant](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://tinyurl.com/GJDCGrant&authuser=7)

* **Rationale:** Because Utica Security is headquartered in Jamaica, Queens, it is perfectly positioned geographically to capture this local grant to fund commercial expansion and offset overhead.

**3\. NYC Customized Training Grant Program**

* **Funder:** NYC Department of Small Business Services (SBS)  
* **Eligibility:** For-profit businesses based in NYC operating for over one year; must train at least 5 employees (businesses with fewer than 5 employees can apply as a consortium).  
* **Funding Amount:** Reimbursement of up to 60% of eligible training costs, with awards ranging from $10,000 to $400,000.  
* **Deadline:** Multiple evaluation cycles scheduled throughout 2026\.  
* **Application URL:** [https://nyc-business.nyc.gov/nycbusiness/business-services/recruitment-and-training/customized-training-grant-program](https://nyc-business.nyc.gov/nycbusiness/business-services/recruitment-and-training/customized-training-grant-program)  
* **Rationale:** Drastically reduces the overhead cost of maintaining state-mandated security guard training and up-skilling your staff on advanced IdentoGO or Pearson VUE technologies.

**4\. Verizon Small Business Digital Ready Grant**

* **Funder:** Verizon and Local Initiatives Support Corporation (LISC)  
* **Eligibility:** For-profit small businesses based in the U.S. Applicants must register and complete two free online courses or events on the Digital Ready platform to unlock the application.  
* **Funding Amount:** $10,000.  
* **Deadline:** Rolling monthly evaluations from June through December 2026 (apply once to remain eligible all year).  
* **Application URL:** [https://digitalready.verizonwireless.com/funding](https://digitalready.verizonwireless.com/funding)  
* **Rationale:** An ideal fit for upgrading digital infrastructure at Utica's fingerprinting/testing center or enhancing digital marketing for its specialized security and executive protection services.

**5\. CitizensNYC Neighborhood Business Grant**

* **Funder:** Citizens Committee for New York City  
* **Eligibility:** NYC-based for-profit businesses operating for at least two years with 10 or fewer employees; must propose a project that improves community quality of life.

* **Funding Amount:** Up to $5,000.

* **Deadline:** The 2026 Cycle 2 deadline is July 2026\.

* **Application URL:** [https://www.citizensnyc.org/nbg/](https://www.citizensnyc.org/nbg/)

* **Rationale:** Utica can architect a proposal around hosting subsidized fingerprinting or defensive driving awareness events for local Queens residents, fulfilling the grant's community impact mandate while generating subsidized revenue.

**6\. Supplier Innovative Finance Program**

* **Funder:** NYS Empire State Development (ESD) and Founders First Capital Partners  
* **Eligibility:** B2B or B2G businesses headquartered in NY generating $250,000 to $10 million in revenue, with an active or pending M/WBE, VOSB, or similar socio-economic certification.  
* **Funding Amount:** $5,000 unrestricted grant, plus access to funder matchmaking.  
* **Deadline:** Rolling / Ongoing.  
* **Application URL:** [https://funding.foundersfirstcapitalpartners.com/nyesd-sifp](https://funding.foundersfirstcapitalpartners.com/nyesd-sifp)  
* **Rationale:** Capitalizes on Utica Security's existing status on the NYC M/WBE list, providing a cash injection and direct matchmaking networking for larger municipal security contracts.

**7\. Security Industry Association (SIA) RISE Scholarship** -- not applicable for 2026 to 2027

* **Funder:** Security Industry Association (SIA)  
* **Eligibility:** Young professionals (SIA student members or RISE members) employed by SIA member companies with at least one year of industry experience. For-profit employees are fully eligible.  
* **Funding Amount:** $3,000 per recipient.  
* **Deadline:** Applications opening shortly for upcoming 2026 cycles.  
* **Application URL:** [https://www.securityindustry.org/professional-development/scholarships/rise-scholarships/](https://www.securityindustry.org/professional-development/scholarships/rise-scholarships/)  
* **Rationale:** Subsidizes the cost of advanced certifications or professional development for Utica's guard supervisors, directly elevating the firm's executive protection and security consulting expertise.

**8\. City of Utica MWBE Grant Program** -- not applicable ot in the area of (Utica)

* **Funder:** City of Utica Urban & Economic Development  
* **Eligibility:** For-profit Minority and Women-Owned Businesses (MWBE) with 10 or fewer employees located *physically within the City of Utica*.  
* **Funding Amount:** $5,000 to $15,000.  
* **Deadline:** Cycles periodically announced (most recent round closed April 2026).  
* **Application URL:** [https://www.cityofutica.com/departments/urban-and-economic-development/business/MWBE-grant-program/index](https://www.cityofutica.com/departments/urban-and-economic-development/business/MWBE-grant-program/index)  
* **Rationale:** While Utica Security is currently headquartered in Jamaica (Queens) , if the firm decides to expand its geographic footprint and open a branch in Utica or Oneida County, this grant offers targeted funding specifically for MWBE equipment purchases and working capital.
