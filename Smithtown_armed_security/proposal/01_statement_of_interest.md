## 1. STATEMENT OF INTEREST AND FIRM OVERVIEW

Utica Security Services Inc. is a New York State licensed, bonded, and fully insured security firm with over five years of continuous experience delivering professional armed and unarmed security services to public-facing, government, and institutional clients. We are pleased to submit this proposal in response to Smithtown Central School District RFP #26/27-01, Armed Security Guard Services, and to express our strong interest in serving as the District's trusted armed security partner.

### 1.1 Statement of Interest

Utica Security Services Inc. understands the gravity of the responsibility entrusted to an armed security contractor operating within a public school district. The safety of students, faculty, staff, and the broader Smithtown community is the central obligation that governs every element of our proposed approach. We do not view this engagement as a routine guard service assignment. We view it as a community protection mission that demands the highest caliber of armed professionals, rigorous training, disciplined operations, and an unwavering commitment to the District's safety culture.

Our Proposer is fully licensed by New York State to provide armed security guard services and complies with New York General Business Law Articles 7 and 7A, including the Security Guard Act of 1992. Every armed guard assigned to the District will hold a valid New York State firearm license and will meet the District's requirement for a minimum of five years of current or prior military and/or law enforcement experience. We recognize that prior private-sector security experience does not satisfy this requirement, and our personnel selection process is designed accordingly.

We are committed to partnering with the Smithtown Central School District, its administration, building principals, and Director of Security to integrate seamlessly into the District's overall security strategy, respect the confidentiality of building-level safety plans, and deliver continuous, professional armed security coverage across all twelve District locations throughout the 2026–2027 school year and each renewal period thereafter.

### 1.2 Firm Overview: Size, Structure, and Staffing Levels

Utica Security Services Inc. is a New York State licensed and insured security services firm with over five years of continuous operational experience delivering professional guard services to public, private, and government clients. The firm maintains a scalable workforce of trained and licensed security professionals capable of supporting continuous multi-site operations, including high-visibility public environments and institutional settings requiring armed coverage.

**Years of Operation:** Over five (5) years of continuous service in New York State

**Workforce:** Over 100 licensed New York State security personnel, supported by supervisory, operations, and administrative staff, enabling sustained coverage across multiple facilities simultaneously

**Core Services:** Armed Security Guard Services, Site Supervision and 24/7 On-Call Management, Access Control and Patrol, Incident Response and Reporting, Staffing Coverage Integrity and Performance Oversight

**Operational Readiness:** 24/7 staffing capability, on-call management, scalable deployment for special events and emergency conditions

The firm maintains staffing levels specifically structured to support continuous operations across multiple locations, including peak periods, service interruptions, and emergency conditions. To ensure uninterrupted coverage, Utica Security maintains dedicated site-based personnel for consistency and familiarity, trained relief and float staff to cover absences or call-outs, and on-call supervisory coverage for incidents and emergencies.

### 1.3 New York State Licensing and Compliance

Utica Security Services Inc. is a New York State licensed and bonded security guard service authorized to provide private security services in New York. The firm meets all requirements of New York General Business Law Articles 7 and 7A, including the Security Guard Act of 1992. All security personnel assigned to the District are licensed by New York State to carry a firearm, and all licenses are maintained current throughout the agreement. License cancellation or revocation of any assigned individual results in immediate removal from the assignment.

The Proposer and all assigned personnel maintain all licenses and permits required by law throughout the full term of this contract and any renewal periods.

### 1.4 Litigation Disclosure

Utica Security Services Inc. affirms that it has not been a party to any civil, criminal, administrative, or arbitration proceedings within the past three years. There are no pending, threatened, or unresolved legal actions involving the firm that would impact its financial stability, regulatory standing, operational capacity, or ability to perform the services required under RFP #26/27-01. This disclosure is provided in full transparency and reflects the firm's commitment to professional conduct and risk-managed operations.

### 1.5 Staff Roles and Responsibilities

Utica Security Services Inc. maintains a clear, scalable, and fully accountable staffing structure dedicated to supporting the safety and operational needs of the Smithtown Central School District. At the executive level, the Chief Executive Officer maintains ultimate contractual accountability to the District, ensuring that performance standards, regulatory compliance, and service expectations are consistently met or exceeded. The Director of Operations provides centralized oversight of all District security operations, with direct responsibility for staffing adequacy, deployment integrity, and operational performance across all twelve school locations. Day-to-day operations are managed by an Operations Manager, supported by dedicated Site and Field Supervisors who provide 24/7 on-call coverage. Supervisors serve as the immediate point of contact for incident response, attendance verification, post-order enforcement, and real-time coordination with District representatives.

Frontline armed security services are delivered by armed guards who are specifically prepared for school environments. Officers perform exterior patrols, vehicle-based station duty, access area monitoring, foot patrols as directed, incident response, and detailed reporting while maintaining a visible, professional, and reassuring presence that enhances school safety and public confidence.

The Human Resources Manager and Recruitment function ensure that only fully vetted, licensed, and background-cleared personnel are assigned to the District, while maintaining a ready pipeline of qualified replacement staff to address absenteeism or surge requirements. The Training Manager oversees all initial, on-the-job, firearms, and in-service training to ensure continuous compliance and skill development aligned with District and RFP requirements.
