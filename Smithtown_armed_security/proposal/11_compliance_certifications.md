## 11. COMPLIANCE AND CERTIFICATIONS

### 11.1 Prevailing Wage Compliance

Utica Security Services Inc. fully complies with New York State Labor Law prevailing wage requirements applicable to this contract. The Proposer acknowledges that prevailing wage rates under Suffolk County PRC #2026900471 apply to all armed security guard services performed under this agreement. The current Suffolk County Article 9 Armed Security Guard prevailing wage rate is $33.93 per hour with supplemental benefits of $2.46 per hour (July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2026); the Proposer will verify any updated schedule before final submission.

The Proposer commits to:
* Paying all assigned armed guards at no less than the applicable prevailing wage rate
* Submitting certified payroll with all invoices
* Submitting the first payroll transcript within 30 days after issuance of the first payroll
* Submitting payroll transcripts every 30 days thereafter
* Understanding that no payment will be made by the District unless certified payroll has been submitted

Invoices are submitted monthly and include time logs, attendance records, types of services rendered, and fees payable.

### 11.2 Drug and Alcohol Free Workplace

Utica Security Services Inc. fully complies with the District's Drug and Alcohol Free Workplace policy. All contractor employees are prohibited from possessing or being under the influence of illegal drugs, controlled substances, drug paraphernalia, designer drugs, alcohol, and tobacco products at or in vehicles under school jurisdiction. Any violation of this policy results in the immediate removal of the affected employee from the District assignment.

### 11.3 Sexual Harassment Prevention

Utica Security Services Inc. has implemented a written sexual harassment prevention policy that meets or exceeds the requirements of New York State Labor Law Section 201-g. All principals and employees have completed required sexual harassment prevention training within the past 12 months. Annual training is provided to all employees. A Sexual Harassment Prevention Certification Form will be executed and submitted with the proposal package as required.

### 11.4 Non-Collusive Certification

The Proposer certifies that the prices and terms contained in this proposal have been determined independently, without collusion, consultation, communication, or agreement with any other Proposer or potential Proposer for the purpose of restricting competition. The Proposer further certifies that it has not disclosed the contents of this proposal to any other person or entity prior to the opening of proposals and has not attempted to induce any other party to submit a proposal for the purpose of restricting competition. A Non-Collusive Certification will be executed and submitted with the proposal package as required.

### 11.5 Iran Divestment Act

The Proposer certifies that it and all proposed subcontractors are not identified on the New York State Prohibited Entities List pursuant to the Iran Divestment Act of 2012. An Iran Divestment Act Certification of Compliance will be executed and submitted with the proposal package as required.

### 11.6 Conflict of Interest

The Proposer certifies that it is of lawful age, is the sole interested party in this proposal, has not colluded with or defrauded the District in any manner, and that no officer or employee of the District has a direct or indirect interest in this proposal or in the contract that may be entered into based upon it. All prices provided are net and exclusive of applicable taxes. A Conflict of Interest Certification will be executed and submitted with the proposal package as required.

### 11.7 Subcontracting

The Proposer will not assign or subcontract any part of this contract without the prior written approval of the Smithtown Central School District. Any approved subcontractor operates under Utica Security Services Inc.'s direct supervision and chain of command and is held to the same licensing, training, background check, SAVE clearance, and conduct standards as directly employed personnel.

### 11.8 Contract Terms Acknowledgment

Utica Security Services Inc. acknowledges and accepts the following contract terms as set forth in RFP #26/27-01:

**Contract Duration and Renewal:** The initial contract term runs from the date of award through June 30, 2027 unless terminated earlier. The Proposer understands that the Board of Education may renew the contract for additional one-year periods not to exceed five years total, under the same terms and conditions. Annual price adjustments, if any, are limited by New York State property tax cap law and the CPI-U New York–Northern NJ May index (not to exceed two percent). Any request for a price increase must be submitted in writing no less than sixty days before the applicable anniversary date. If the CPI-U results in a negative index, an equivalent reduction applies.

**Non-Exclusive Contract:** The Proposer understands that this contract is non-exclusive and that the District retains the right to procure similar services from other sources during the term of the agreement.

**Binding Condition:** The Proposer understands that no binding contract is created until the District has approved funds for the purpose and the Board of Education has approved the contract award.

**Addenda Acknowledgment:** The Proposer acknowledges receipt of all addenda issued to RFP #26/27-01 prior to the proposal submission deadline. The Proposer understands that only written clarifications issued as formal addenda by the District are binding; no oral interpretations are controlling.

**Termination:** The Proposer acknowledges that the District may terminate this contract immediately upon written notice for cause, including failure to perform required services or comply with contract provisions. The District may also terminate for convenience upon seven days' written notice. Termination may result in removal from future proposal lists for an indeterminate period.

**Employee Removal:** The Proposer acknowledges that the District may, with or without cause, remove or request the removal of any contractor employee from the District assignment. The Proposer will comply with any such request without delay and will not reassign a removed individual to any District location without prior District approval.

**Damage Responsibility:** The Proposer accepts full responsibility for any damage to District property or facilities caused by the negligence or actions of its employees or vehicles. The Proposer will repair or replace any such damage at its own cost and without delay.

**Annual Attestation:** The Proposer acknowledges the requirement to complete and submit the District's annual contractor certification for each year of the contract and for each renewal period.

**Office Availability:** Utica Security Services Inc. maintains competent management representatives available twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week, with full authority to handle contract matters, respond to District inquiries, and initiate corrective action.
