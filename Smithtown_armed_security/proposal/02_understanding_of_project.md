## 2. UNDERSTANDING OF THE PROJECT

### 2.1 Project Scope and District Needs

Utica Security Services Inc. has thoroughly reviewed RFP #26/27-01 and understands that the Smithtown Central School District requires uniformed armed security guard services at twelve District locations throughout the 2026–2027 school year, with the potential for summer coverage and authorized District events outside standard school hours. The District's primary objective is the protection of school buildings, grounds, and occupants from unlawful entry that could result in bodily harm.

The Proposer fully understands the scope of services, the District's authority to modify hours, relocate guards anywhere on school grounds, and order or reduce services at its sole discretion. The Proposer accepts that payment will be made only for services actually rendered, that no travel time will be paid, and that no reimbursement will be provided for fuel or personal mileage. The Proposer is fully prepared to provide proposer-supplied vehicles or maintain appropriate automobile insurance on personal vehicles used while guards are stationed outside buildings, consistent with RFP requirements.

The Proposer understands that guards are expected to be primarily stationed outside school buildings unless the District directs otherwise, and that coverage must remain continuous during lunch breaks and rest periods. Additional armed guards will cover breaks when needed to maintain uninterrupted post coverage at all locations.

The Proposer understands that building-level safety plans and emergency response protocols are strictly confidential. All assigned personnel are trained to treat this information with the highest level of discretion and to neither discuss nor disclose any District-specific security information outside of authorized channels.

### 2.3 Operational Requirements and Protocols

The Proposer understands and accepts the following operational requirements in full:

**Continuity of Coverage:** Coverage remains continuous during all lunch breaks and rest periods. The Proposer maintains a float and relief pool specifically to ensure no post goes uncovered during any break or absence.

**Same-Guard Continuity:** The Proposer assigns the same guards to each building as much as possible to promote familiarity with staff, students, building layouts, and emergency procedures.

**Communications:** All assigned guards carry company-issued cellular devices and a two-way radio or other mobile communication method at all times while on duty.

**Uniforms:** District-approved uniforms are required while performing duties. The Proposer coordinates with the District before initial deployment to confirm uniform specifications and obtain approval.

**Conduct:** Contractor employees do not fraternize with students. Smoking is prohibited anywhere on school grounds. Personnel do not create a nuisance, interfere with students or employees, destroy property, or enter unauthorized areas except in life-or-death emergencies.

**Log Procedures:** At the start of each duty assignment, personnel sign the log book, read all entries since the previous shift, review any problems or incidents, and confer with supervisors regarding any special instructions.

**Witness Obligations:** The Proposer acknowledges that contractor employees may be required to appear as witnesses in administrative, civil, or criminal proceedings related to incidents occurring during the performance of services, and all assigned personnel are prepared to fulfill this obligation.

**District Meetings:** The Proposer attends District meetings as requested regarding services or incidents.

### 2.4 Incident Reporting and Coordination

All incidents are immediately reported to appropriate public authorities as required, and to District officials without delay. The Proposer provides:

* **Verbal incident reports** to the Business Official and/or Director of Security by close of shift
* **Written incident reports** on Utica Security Services Inc. company letterhead to the Business Official and/or Director of Security

The Proposer coordinates all services with District representatives and adheres to the District's overall security strategy at all times.
