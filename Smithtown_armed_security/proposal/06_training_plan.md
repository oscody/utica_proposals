## 6. TRAINING PLAN

### 6.1 Training Philosophy

Utica Security Services Inc. maintains a comprehensive training program designed to ensure that all armed security personnel assigned to the Smithtown Central School District are fully prepared to perform their duties safely, professionally, and in compliance with all contractual, regulatory, and operational requirements. Our training philosophy in the school district context emphasizes:

* Student and staff safety above all other priorities
* Professional conduct appropriate to an educational environment
* De-escalation as the preferred first response to any behavioral incident
* Regulatory compliance with all NYS and federal requirements governing armed security
* Continuous firearms proficiency and active threat preparedness
* Strict adherence to the District's chain of command and safety protocols

All training is documented in each assigned guard's personnel file. Training certificates for any additional training are provided to the District within five days of completion. Failure to complete required firearms training is grounds for immediate removal from the District assignment.

### 6.2 Initial and On-the-Job Training

**8-Hour Pre-Assignment Training (Initial Course)**

All guards complete the following pre-assignment training before their first day of service at any District location:

| Subject | Hours |
|---|---|
| Introduction to the Security Guard Profession; NYS roles and ethical standards | 1 |
| Legal Powers and Limitations; Penal Law Article 35; Use of Force Basics | 1 |
| Emergency Situations and Response: active shooter, lockdown, fire, medical, bomb threat | 1 |
| Communications and De-Escalation; incident reporting basics; radio protocols | 1 |
| Access Control and Patrol Procedures; situational awareness; exterior station procedures | 1 |
| Ethics and Professional Conduct; student interaction boundaries; confidentiality | 1 |
| Terrorism Awareness; suspicious indicators; reporting protocols | 1 |
| Employment Standards and Licensing; NYS compliance; documentation requirements | 1 |

**16-Hour On-the-Job Training (School-Specific)**

Following pre-assignment training, all guards complete 16 hours of on-the-job training aligned to the District's specific environment:

| Subject | Hours |
|---|---|
| District Orientation: school layout, daily schedules, building contacts, post orders | 2 |
| School Environment Protocols: student arrival/dismissal, break coverage, log procedures | 2 |
| Post Orders, Chain of Command, and District Compliance Expectations | 2 |
| Exterior Station and Vehicle Patrol Techniques; foot patrol procedures | 2 |
| De-Escalation, Use of Force, and Behavioral Management in a School Setting | 3 |
| Emergency Response and Incident Coordination: lockdown, evacuation, medical, active shooter | 3 |
| Reporting: verbal shift-end report, written reports on company letterhead, log documentation | 2 |

**Annual 8-Hour In-Service Training**

All guards assigned to the District complete an annual 8-hour in-service training that updates and refreshes all pre-assignment and school-specific content, incorporates any changes to District protocols, and incorporates any updated NYS legal or regulatory requirements.

### 6.3 Firearms Training Program

Utica Security Services Inc. provides a rigorous firearms training program that meets and exceeds all applicable legal requirements and best industry practice, whichever is more frequent.

**Quarterly Firearms Training:** The Proposer provides at least one firearms training session per quarter for all armed guards assigned to the District. Quarterly training includes live-fire qualification, safe handling and maintenance, situational judgment exercises, and review of applicable legal standards under NY Penal Law Article 35 and General Business Law.

**Annual Firearms Refresher:** Firearms training refreshes at least annually and as often as required by applicable law or best industry practice.

All firearms used by assigned guards are maintained in good working order at all times. The Proposer bears all costs for firearms, ammunition, licensing, training, and maintenance. Certificates of completed firearms training are provided to the District within five days of each training session completion.

### 6.4 Active Shooter Tactical Training

The Proposer provides at least bi-annual active shooter tactical training for all armed guards assigned to the District. This training includes:

* Active shooter response protocols aligned to law enforcement and Department of Homeland Security guidance
* Threat identification, isolation, and neutralization within the context of an occupied school building
* Communication and coordination with District administration and law enforcement during an active threat event
* Evacuation support and reunification procedures
* Trauma-informed response to students and staff during and after a critical incident
* Scenario-based exercises simulating school-specific active threat environments

Training records for active shooter tactical training are maintained in each assigned guard's personnel file. The Proposer provides the District with documentation of completed sessions within five days of each training completion.

### 6.5 Suffolk County Police Department Coordination

The Proposer understands the District's expectation that the successful Proposer train with the Suffolk County Police Department or Town Safety. Utica Security Services Inc. commits to actively coordinating with the Suffolk County Police Department and/or applicable Town Safety units to schedule and participate in joint training exercises and familiarization sessions for all armed guards assigned to the District. This coordination enhances interoperability between District security personnel and local law enforcement during any incident requiring law enforcement response and is initiated during the pre-service mobilization period before July 1, 2026.

### 6.6 Training Recordkeeping and Reporting

The Proposer maintains complete training records for all assigned personnel. Upon District request, the following training documentation is provided without charge:

* 8-hour pre-assignment training certificate
* 16-hour on-the-job training certificate (NYS mandated)
* Security Guard Training Certificate
* Annual in-service training certificates
* Quarterly firearms training certificates
* Bi-annual active shooter tactical training certificates
* Any additional specialized training certificates, provided within five days of completion

Training records for all assigned individuals are available within the employee files provided to the District upon request.
