## 7. LICENSING, BACKGROUND CHECKS, AND SAVE CLEARANCE

### 7.1 NYS Licensing Requirements

All armed guards assigned to the Smithtown Central School District hold a valid New York State security guard license and a valid New York State license to carry a firearm. The Proposer verifies the current status of all licenses before initial assignment and monitors license status continuously throughout the contract term. Any license cancellation, revocation, or failure to maintain a valid license results in the immediate removal of the affected guard from the District assignment.

The Proposer maintains all required New York State licenses and registrations for the security business throughout the full term of this agreement and any renewal periods. Evidence of business licensing and registration is provided to the District upon request.

### 7.2 Pre-Employment Background Checks

All armed guards assigned to the District have successfully completed a comprehensive pre-employment background check before assignment. Background checks include:

- Fingerprinting
- Criminal records search (state and federal)
- Previous employment verification
- Social Security number verification
- DMV records check
- Drug screening

Background check results are maintained in each individual's employee file. Upon District request, the Proposer provides the relevant employee files without charge, including employment application, training records, NYS Security Guard License, NYS registration evidence, birth certificate or I-9 or valid NYS DMV driver's license, background check results, and drug test results.

### 7.3 SAVE Clearance Compliance

The Proposer fully complies with New York State SAVE legislation. The security business and all armed guards assigned to the District are cleared by the New York State Education Department before any assignment begins. The Proposer bears all costs for SAVE clearances and all associated administrative expenses. No guard is deployed to any District location without a current and valid SAVE clearance on file.

### 7.4 Drug Screening Program

All armed guards assigned to the District complete a pre-employment drug screening before initial assignment. Annual drug screenings are conducted for all armed guards assigned to the District throughout the contract term, at the Proposer's expense. Documentation of successful drug testing is included in each assigned individual's professional biography and employee file. Successful drug testing documentation is provided to the District as part of the annual individual biographical update.

### 7.5 Personnel File Availability

Annually, and at any time upon District request, the Proposer provides individual biographies for each assigned guard identifying the individual, their prior work experience, proof of firearms and security training, and proof of current NYS registration. The Superintendent may approve or reject any assigned individual at the District's sole discretion. Replacement of any individual requires prior District approval before the replacement begins any assignment.

The Proposer provides photo identification badges for all personnel deployed on District property.
