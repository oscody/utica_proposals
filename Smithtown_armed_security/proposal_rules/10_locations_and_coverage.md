Coverage assumptions from the RFP:

- Fourteen armed guards are required on each school day unless otherwise noted.
- Two guards are required at Smithtown High School East.
- Two guards are required at Smithtown High School West.
- One guard is required at each remaining listed school.
- General hours include 30 minutes before and 30 minutes after school day.
- High school general coverage hours: 7:00 AM to 2:30 PM.
- Middle school general coverage hours: 7:30 AM to 3:00 PM.
- Elementary school general coverage hours: 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
- District may increase or decrease approximate minutes or hours.
