# Response Package Checklist Rules

The proposal package should include all RFP-required response materials. Mark missing company-specific information as `TO BE PROVIDED`.

- Proposal must use the RFP's Proposal Form; alternate forms are not acceptable.
- Proposal must include one original and two copies.
- Proposal must complete all forms and sign in ink in all required places.
- Pricing must be entered in the designated fields on the Proposal Sheet.
- Prices must be net and inclusive of all labor and associated charges.
- Where required, sums must be expressed in both words and figures; written words govern if there is a discrepancy.
- Proposal must include legal proposer name and entity type.
- Proposal must be signed by a person legally authorized to bind the proposer.
- Corporation proposals must identify state of incorporation and use the corporate seal on signature pages where required.
- Agent-signed proposals must include a current Power of Attorney.
- Proposal must not include stipulations, qualifications, limitations, amendments, omissions, or deviations from RFP requirements.
- Supplemental agreements needed to perform the services must be included and remain subject to District counsel review and Board approval.
- Proposal should include adequate brochures, latest printed specifications, advertising literature, and relevant website information where applicable.
- Proposal should include any addenda acknowledgement required by the Proposal Form.
- Proposal must include all required certifications and notarized forms identified in the RFP.
- Proposal must include proof of qualifications, insurance capability, licenses, bonding, training, references, and financial responsibility as required.
- Upon District request, proposer must provide four written copies of updated financial responsibility evidence and insurance certificates within three working days of proposal opening.
