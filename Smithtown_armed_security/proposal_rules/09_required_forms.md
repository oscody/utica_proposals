# Required Forms And Certifications Rules

Use this as the proposal forms checklist. If drafting form responses, preserve the required form names and signature/notary requirements.

- Statement of Proposer's Qualifications.
- Information Sheet.
- References, listing five accounts similar in size and scope to the District.
- Conflict of Interest Certification.
- Non-Collusive Certification.
- Iran Divestment Act Certification of Compliance, or Declaration of Proposer's Inability to Provide Certification if applicable.
- Hold Harmless Agreement, signed and notarized, submitted with proposal.
- Sexual Harassment Prevention Certification Form.
- Form of Proposal.
- School Locations acknowledgement or use as operations reference.
- Prevailing Wage Rate Schedule acknowledgement/compliance.
- Proposer Response Form, if included or requested in final package.
- Form of Agreement, for informational purposes unless final execution is required.
- Successful Contractor's Attestation/Certification, for informational purposes and annual post-award compliance.
- W-9 Form.

Certification content rules:

- Conflict of Interest Certification must state the proposer is lawful age, sole interested party, no collusion/fraud, no District board/officer/employee interest, RFP documents examined, and prices are net/exclusive of applicable taxes.
- Non-Collusive Certification must certify independent pricing, no improper disclosure before opening, and no attempt to induce another party to submit a proposal to restrict competition.
- Iran Divestment certification must state proposer/contractor and proposed subcontractors are not identified on the Prohibited Entities List.
- Sexual Harassment certification must state proposer has implemented a written policy, provides annual training, and principals/employees completed training in the last 12 months, meeting Labor Law Section 201-g.
- Hold Harmless Agreement must indemnify and defend the District, Board, officers, agents, servants, and employees, and survive completion or termination.
