# New York's 2026 security guard RFP landscape for Utica

The 2026 NY security guard procurement market is dominated by two master vehicles — **NYS OGS Award 23106** (expiring April 23, 2028) and **DCAS Citywide Contract 85724P0003** (just awarded April 7, 2026) — which together absorb most state and city agency demand into mini-RFQs rather than standalone RFPs. The highest-probability opportunities for Utica in 2026 are therefore **(1) the NYCHA 503229 re-compete, (2) rolling enrollment on the DCAS NPSSGRP Qualified Provider List, (3) subcontracting to Universal Protection / Aron Security under 85724P0003, (4) a likely NYCEDC Citywide re-solicitation, and (5) a probable 2026 re-bid of Javits Center event security and Trust for Governors Island security**. Several confirmed open 2026 solicitations are identified below, alongside a dense pipeline of contracts aging toward re-bid and specific pre-qualification actions to take now.

The research found no publicly posted 2026 RFPs from Northwell, NYU Langone, Mount Sinai, NewYork-Presbyterian, Montefiore, MSK, Central Park Conservancy, Hudson River Park Trust, most BIDs, or the Archdiocese/Brooklyn/Rockville Centre dioceses — these procure through closed supplier portals or direct referral, so the play is **registration and relationship-building**, not RFP response. Public-sector guard RFPs in NY overwhelmingly live inside BidNet Direct (paywalled), NYSCR (login-walled), and PASSPort — without registration on all three, Utica is effectively invisible to the market.

## Category 2 — Private and religious schools, with NPSSGRP as the main gate

The **Non-Public School Security Guard Reimbursement Program (NPSSGRP)** is the highest-leverage 2026 channel for Utica given its in-house training and fingerprinting infrastructure. Administered by **NYC DCAS** under RFQ **PIN# 85617QL001**, the Qualified Provider List is **open-ended with rolling applications** and currently lists **84 providers** as of March 2026. Crucially, **Local Law enacted December 2024 eliminated the 300-student minimum**, making every non-public school in NYC eligible regardless of size and opening roughly 15,000 additional students of coverage capacity — new vendor slots are opening now. Program page: https://www.nyc.gov/site/dcas/business/school-security-guards-security-vendors.page. Current QPL: https://www.nyc.gov/assets/dcas/downloads/pdf/business/security-guard-qualified-provider-list.pdf. Questions to Security4Schools@dcas.nyc.gov. Enrollment requires active PASSPort status, Article 7-A licensing, prevailing wage compliance, overtime ≤15% of non-OT hours, and a **25% markup cap** on DCAS reimbursement.

**Agudath Israel of America** (Rabbi Yeruchim Silber, Director of NY Government Relations) is the dominant referral intermediary connecting yeshivas in Boro Park, Williamsburg, Crown Heights, Flatbush, Queens, Monsey, and Rockland — not a contracting entity itself but the network that brings new NPSSGRP-approved vendors to schools: https://agudah.org/nonpublic-school-security-guard-reimbursement-program. **Torah Umesorah** plays a similar role across day schools.

Diocesan procurement is conducted school-by-school through business managers and diocesan finance offices — **no public RFPs**. Direct outreach targets: Archdiocese of New York (archny.org, Finance Office + Superintendent of Schools), Diocese of Brooklyn (dioceseofbrooklyn.org), and **Diocese of Rockville Centre** with named procurement contacts **Judy Avitabile (516-678-5800 x532, javitabile@drvc.org), Martin Dwonarski (x205, mdwonarski@drvc.org), and Lawrence Egan (x519, legan@drvc.org)**. Independent NYSAIS schools (Collegiate, Dalton, Trinity, Spence, Horace Mann, Riverdale, ECFS, Brearley, Grace Church, Poly Prep, Saint Ann's, Packer, SAR, Ramaz, HAFTR, North Shore Hebrew Academy, Westchester Day) procure quietly through Directors of Operations/Facilities.

Adjacent funding driver: the **NYS Nonprofit Security Grant (SCAHC) 2026 RFA** and the **NYSED Nonpublic School Safety Equipment (NPSE) grant** (~$70M in 2025) fund hardening and drive pull-through demand for guard services. SCAHC 2026 RFA: https://criminaljustice.ny.gov/ofpa/SCAHC%202026%20RFA%20FINAL%204.10.26%20(1)%20(1).pdf.

## Category 5 — Healthcare security is mostly closed-sourced

No publicly posted 2026 hospital guard RFPs surfaced for Northwell, NYU Langone, Mount Sinai, NewYork-Presbyterian, Montefiore, MSK, HSS, URMC, Rochester Regional, Kaleida, ECMC, Catholic Health Buffalo, Albany Med, St. Peter's, Bassett, Stony Brook Medicine, SUNY Upstate, Crouse, St. Joseph's Syracuse, Nuvance, Garnet, Richmond University Medical, Maimonides, Brookdale, Interfaith, or Kingsbrook. **Hospitals run closed sourcing through Jaggaer, Workday Strategic Sourcing, Coupa, and GHX supplier portals**, and the large systems have shifted heavily to in-house W-2 security officer forces. The realistic play is **supplier enrollment + subcontracting flex/event/overflow staffing to Allied Universal, Securitas, GardaWorld, and HSS Inc.** NYC H+H still issues competitive RFPs case-by-case (https://www.nychealthandhospitals.org/vendor-resources/) and is consolidating security procurement as the **Maimonides integration** progresses. The $324.7M H+H HERRC migrant-shelter security package (Garda World, Mulligan, Protos) is being **wound down, not re-solicited**, following Mayor Mamdani's January 2026 Emergency Executive Order phasing out HERRC within 45 days; remaining needs are absorbing into DHS/DSS shelter procurement. Required credentials across hospital systems: **IAHSS CHPA/CPP, BLS/CPR, CPI/Handle-With-Care de-escalation, HIPAA, NY DOS 8+16+8-hour training, F-01 Fire Guard in NYC, FSD in high-rise medical offices, $5M CGL and $2M Security E&O**.

## Category 6 — Facility-management subcontracting is the fastest volume path

Register simultaneously on all eight major FM prime portals: **ABM** (Ariba-based ABM Sourcing), **CBRE** (Service Partner Portal), **JLL** (Jaggaer Supplier Network), **Cushman & Wakefield** (Coupa), **Sodexo** (Coupa — healthcare/education), **Aramark** (stadiums/venues), **ISS World**, and **Compass/Eurest**. These primes hold integrated facilities management contracts across Hudson Yards, One World Trade Center, Brookfield Place, SL Green, Vornado, Tishman Speyer, Rudin, Durst, RXR, Related, and Empire State Realty Trust portfolios, and they subcontract security overflow and specialty roles continuously. **DHS shelter providers** (non-profits) prime the shelter contracts and subcontract security, making them another critical channel in the post-HERRC transition. **DASNY construction contracts** bundle site security into prime GC awards — monitor NYSCR subcontracting advertisements (Lanmark Group, LeChase, Quintal Contracting visible on 4/14/2026) for subcontract entry. **Brooklyn Navy Yard** completed a 2025 Security Master Plan consulting engagement and employs in-house officers; not currently a contract target. **NYC School Construction Authority** bundles site security inside GC contracts: http://www.nycsca.org/Doing-Business/Contracting-with-Us.

## Category 7 — FDNY-certified roles are a direct B2B sell, not an RFP market

FDNY Certificate of Fitness demand is **not driven by RFPs** but by direct procurement from landlords, GCs, hotels, shelters, and hospitals. **F-01 Fire Guard** commands $25–30/hour premium pricing; **F-80/F-89** drive event and outdoor-assembly staffing (Governors Ball, Tribeca, Comic Con, NYFW, Electric Zoo, Macy's Parade); **F-58/S-95** support sprinkler/standpipe outages on construction sites; **FSD (Fire Safety Director)** is mandated at every Class E high-rise office and hotel 75 feet or taller in NYC. Primary buyers: SL Green, Vornado, Tishman Speyer, Rudin, Durst, Brookfield, RXR, Related, and Empire State Realty Trust for FSDs; Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, IHG, and Accor for hotels (typically through ABM/JLL/CBRE/Securitas); and **Turner, Skanska, Tishman/AECOM, Gilbane, Tutor Perini, Lendlease, Suffolk Construction, Structure Tone, Plaza Construction** for construction site fire guards under NYC DOB Local Law 196. The **DCAS PQL000150** open-ended M/WBE Security PQL (for projects ≤$1.5M) is the principal city gateway if Utica is M/WBE-certified (Balduin Mathieu, BMathieu@dcas.nyc.gov, https://a856-cityrecord.nyc.gov/RequestDetail/20240131112).

## What this changes about Utica's go-to-market

The research reframes the 2026 market from "find the RFPs" to "get inside the gates." Standalone open RFPs are scarce because **NY State and NYC have deliberately consolidated procurement** into master vehicles (OGS 23106, DCAS 85724P0003, DCAS NPSSGRP QPL, DCAS PQL000150) plus closed supplier portals at every major hospital and large institution. The implication: Utica's competitive edges in rapid mobilization, in-house training, fingerprinting, and FDNY-certified pipeline are real differentiators but only surface **after** PQL entry, supplier enrollment, and subcontracting relationships are in place. The **NPSSGRP QPL is the single highest-probability 2026 win** given its rolling nature, recent legislative expansion, and precise alignment with Utica's stated strengths. The **OGS 23106 successor RFP in late 2026/early 2027** is the largest strategic prize for an Utica-based firm — its home-region advantage in Regions 5 (Mohawk Valley/Central NY) and adjacent regions makes it the contract to prepare for now, not later.
