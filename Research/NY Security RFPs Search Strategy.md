## **The Regulatory, Economic, and Compliance Framework**

Success in New York State public procurement requires absolute mastery of the underlying economic and regulatory statutes that govern contract execution. A competitively priced, highly technical proposal will be summarily disqualified if statutory forms are incomplete or if wage calculations violate state law.

### **Prevailing Wage Mandates and Margin Compression**

The most significant factor influencing bid competitiveness is the strict enforcement of prevailing wage laws. Under Article 9, Section 230.6 of the New York State Labor Law, contractors providing building service employees—which explicitly includes security guards—must adhere to the prevailing wage schedules published annually by the fiscal officer.10

For the period covering July 2025 through June 2026, the statutory prevailing wage rates for security personnel in key New York jurisdictions are rigidly established. The base hourly cash rate for a Security Guard (Armed) is mandated at $33.89 per hour, while the rate for a Security Guard (Unarmed) is mandated at $22.59 per hour.10 It is critical to understand that these figures represent only the base hourly cash rate of pay; they do not encompass the entirety of the supplemental benefits (such as health insurance, welfare, and pension contributions) that must also be provided to the employee or paid as a direct cash equivalent.

Because labor costs are fixed by statute across all competitors, firms cannot win contracts by simply undercutting the hourly wage of their personnel. Attempting to do so results in immediate disqualification and potential investigation by the Department of Labor. Price differentiation must therefore be achieved through operational efficiency: minimizing administrative overhead, optimizing shift scheduling, and radically reducing the cost of employee turnover. This dynamic amplifies the immense value of Utica Security Services' vertical integration.1 In a prevailing wage environment, controlling the cost of employee acquisition via internal training and fingerprinting is the most effective method for preserving profit margins and allowing for competitive markup rates on overhead and profit during the RFP pricing phase.

### **Diversity Utilization Goals: M/WBE and SDVOB**

The broader global security market is characterized by massive corporate consolidation, with multinational conglomerates possessing immense capital resources.4 However, New York State's procurement framework actively counterbalances this through aggressive Minority and Women-Owned Business Enterprise (M/WBE) and Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Business (SDVOB) utilization mandates.

Agencies such as the New York State Insurance Fund (NYSIF) formally mandate up to 30% M/WBE participation and 6% SDVOB participation on their facility management and security contracts.11 Similarly, the Ralph Wilson Park Conservancy in Buffalo requires 25% MBE and 5% WBE participation.12 If Utica Security Services holds an M/WBE certification, the firm possesses a structural shield against multinational conglomerates, granting exclusive access to designated procurement pools and making the firm a highly sought-after subcontractor for massive prime contractors. If the firm is not certified, pursuing joint ventures with certified entities or actively mentoring M/WBE subcontractors is absolutely essential for winning multi-million dollar state contracts.

### **Mandatory Administrative Protocols**

New York State agencies have implemented stringent, digitized compliance frameworks. The transition away from legacy systems like HHS Accelerator to the comprehensive PASSPort system is fully complete for agencies like NYC DCAS.13 Vendors must ensure their PASSPort profiles are flawlessly updated. Furthermore, agencies require detailed Vendor Responsibility Questionnaires (VRQ) filed electronically via the VendRep system to disclose financial health and litigation history.15 Recently, Executive Order 16 was enacted, requiring all state contracts to include mandatory certification that the vendor does not conduct prohibited business operations in Russia.15 Failure to include an EO 16 certification results in automatic rejection of the bid package.
