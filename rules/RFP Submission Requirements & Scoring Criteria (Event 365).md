Technical Proposal Notices  

A. No aspect of the Technical Proposal shall reference the pricing.  

B. The Technical Proposal shall include:  

1\. A Cover Page with Event Name and Number, Vendor name, address, contact information, and date.  2\. Page numbers and a Table of Contents clearly identifying each section.  

C. Each section below serves as an Evaluation Criteria and will be scored by RGRTA. Weighting is noted in  parenthesis.  

Technical Proposal Sections  

1\. Qualifications, Resources and Experience (15%)  

A. Required Information:  

1\. Vendor introduction and statement of interest  

2\. Identification of proposed property(ies): Transit Center, Main Street Campus, or both  3\. Firm overview: size, structure, staffing levels  

4\. Minimum five (5) years of experience in large, high-volume public (non-secure) facilities  5\. Disclosure of any litigation (civil or criminal) in the past 3 years  

6\. Five (5) comparable engagements (or explanation if fewer)  

7\. List any clients that are government entities and transit agencies  

8\. Subcontractor details (if applicable)  

9\. Staff roles and responsibilities  

10\.Organizational chart including subcontractors  

B. Scoring Considerations:  

1\. Depth and relevance of experience in similar environments  

2\. Demonstrated success with transit or public sector clients  

3\. Stability and capacity of the organization  

4\. Transparency regarding litigation and subcontractor use  

5\. Clarity of organizational structure and staff roles
