**Note: References to security staff applies to all staff assigned to the contract. Specific titles will only  be used when a requirement applies to a specific job title.** 

**1\. Professional Standards** 

A. Security staff shall meet or exceed the professional standards outlined in the Security Officers and Patrol  Services Competency Model developed by the International Foundation for Protection Officers (IFPO).  1\. The model is provided in the Attachments tab within the Event. 

2\. This model identifies the essential competencies required of security roles across all industry sectors  and includes the following key domains: Personal Effectiveness Competencies; Academic  Competencies; and Workplace Competencies.  

**2\. Medical Fitness Standards** 

A. Security staff all security personnel shall successfully pass a medical examination, conducted at the  Vendor’s expense, prior to duty assignment. The examination must confirm that the individual meets the  minimum physical and medical standards necessary to perform essential security functions safely and  effectively.  

B. At a minimum, security staff must demonstrate: 

1\. Freedom from any communicable disease that may pose a risk to others. 

2\. Binocular vision, correctable to 20/20 in both eyes. 

3\. Ability to distinguish shades of color, sufficient for identifying uniforms, badges, and safety indicators. 4\. Unaided ability to hear ordinary conversation at 20 feet, or with hearing aids that do not interfere with  radio or emergency communication equipment. 

5\. Freedom from illegal drug use, verified by a urinalysis or oral swab drug screening. Prescribed  medications must not impair job performance. 

6\. Clear and distinct speech, free from any condition that may impair effective verbal communication. 7\. Freedom from any physical or emotional condition that may inhibit or preclude the ability to meet the  professional standards outlined in this RFP and the resulting contract. 

8\. Normal vital signs, including blood pressure, are within acceptable clinical ranges. 

**3\. Physical Fitness Standards** 

A. Vendors must ensure that security staff are physically capable of performing essential job functions,  including but not limited to:   
1\. Standing or walking for extended periods (up to 8 hours) 

2\. Responding quickly to emergencies (e.g., running short distances, climbing stairs) 

3\. Performing basic physical interventions (e.g., restraining individuals if necessary) 

4\. Lifting or moving objects up to 50 lbs 

B. Vendors must certify that security staff meet these standards through: 

1\. A pre-employment physical capability assessment, or 

2\. A signed attestation from a licensed medical professional within the past 12 months 

C. After the onboarding security staff, Vendor shall certify that they continue to meet the physical fitness  standards every two (2) years.

**4\. Health and Drug Screening Standards** 

A. Drug tests must be conducted within sixty (60) days prior to the security staff’s initial assignment at  Vendor’s expense using either urine or oral fluid (mouth swab) collection methods.

**SECTION 3: TRANSIT CENTER SITE SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS** 

**1\. Overview of the Transit Center** 

A. The Transit Center, located at 60 St. Paul Street, Rochester, NY 14614, features: 

1\. An enclosed, climate-controlled environment; 

2\. 26 indoor bus bays across two busways; 

3\. Public restrooms and customer service desks. 

4\. One (1) outdoor bus shelter adjacent to the building on Mortimer Street. 

B. The Transit Center serves as the primary transfer hub for customers and operates: 

1\. Monday–Friday: 4:30 a.m. – 12:00 a.m. 

2\. Weekends: 5:30 a.m. – 12:00 a.m. 

C. Approximately 10,000 daily customers visit the Transit Center.  

D. Security Staff are part of a broader safety and emergency management strategy that includes physical  security systems and periodic law enforcement presence. Security Staff are expected to integrate with  RGRTA staff and other contracted personnel to maintain a safe, welcoming, and orderly environment.

1\. Provide a visible deterrent to loitering and misconduct. 

2\. Support RGRTA’s goal of proactive, customer-focused security. 

B. Security staff shall engage customers in a polite, professional manner.

1\. All applicable Federal, State, and Local laws and regulations 

2\. Post Orders and directives issued by RGRTA specific to the Transit Center 

3\. The Vendor’s internal policies and procedures

**SECTION 4: POST ORDERS FOR TRANSIT CENTER** 

**1\. General Duties and Conduct** 

A. Report to duty on time and in a complete, appropriate uniform. All Security staff are to be on the floor and  in their assigned area by their scheduled start time. 

B. Do not leave the Transit Center property unless properly relieved or, in the case when no Security staff is  scheduled for relief, the shift is over.  

C. Review post orders, any pass-on book, and recent security/incident reports. 

D. Check roll call room for any posted memos.  

E. Sign out a radio and handcuffs from the Radio Controllers. All radios need to be carried in a radio holster  and handcuffs in a pouch. This should always be done through the window of the break room. Perform a  radio check with the Controller to ensure it is working and to let them know you are on duty F. Inspect post for required equipment and cleanliness 

G. Maintain a collective daily Security Officer Report, maintaining record of every escort, incident, event,  and other activity that goes on during the shift. Security staff are responsible for reporting repeat ban  violators to the on duty RGRTA Supervisors. 

H. Complete incident reports regarding all incidents or situations requiring a report. 

I. Officers are engaged with patrons while working the floor. Greet visitors when making eye contact and  offer help when someone looks as though they need assistance. Customer service is a priority in this field. J. Enforce RGRTA’s Customer Rules of Conduct    
1\. These are provided on the Attachment Tab of the Event. 

K. Physically intervene when it is reasonably necessary to defend themselves or others from imminent harm  or to prevent a crime in progress such as theft or vandalism.

1\. Personal cell phones 

2\. Headphones 

3\. Smartwatches, smart glasses, or Bluetooth devices 

4\. Any media device for personal use 

5\. Drugs, alcohol or any other intoxicating agent 

**3\. Respond to emergencies:** 

A. Police/Fire/Medical Emergencies should be reported to the Controller and an RGRTA Supervisor  immediately. Bring the AED/First Aid kid when responding to a medical emergency or delegate someone  to get it when necessary.   
1\. Unless someone’s life is imminently at risk, all requests for AMR and Police are to be made by Radio  Control. 

**4\. Detect, Deter and Report** 

A. The primary function of Security staff is to detect, deter and report.

B. A visible and active security presence is essential to effective deterrence. Security staff must remain alert,  engaged, and proactive at all times. The objective is to prevent incidents  through early detection and intervention.

**5\. Security Zone Patrol Strategy** 

A. In support of the Security Zone Patrol Strategy, Security Staff shall adhere to the following: 1\. In the event of an all-hands-on deck call, leave their zone to assist. As soon as the incident is under  control, Security Staff are to return to designated areas as quickly as possible. 

2\. Security Staff are to move across designated zones on a prescribed frequency subject to RGRTA  discretion.. 

3\. Security staff must avoid grouping with other officers or supervisors and must not lean on or stand in  front of the customer service desks. 

4\. Security Staff should position themselves with visibility toward populated areas.  

5\. When working in zones C & D, Security Staff should position themselves on an angle that allows them  to view inside the bathrooms.  

6\. Security staff must conduct routine checks of the Mortimer Street shelters three times per hour and  maintain a strong presence by spending a few minutes in the area during each visit. 

**6\. Escalation Procedure** 

A. Security staff will approach any individual who has been viewed wandering, loitering, horse playing,  playing loud music, arguing with another person, appears under the influence, is sitting on the ground, or is  in violation of any other Transit Center rule.  

B. Security staff will initially provide the individual with a warning.  

C. If misconduct continues, Security staff will ask the individual to leave.  

D. If an individual refuses to comply, Security staff will immediately request backup and escort the individual  to the nearest exit, clearly informing them they are not permitted to return for the remainder of the day. 1\. Prior to initiating the escort, Security staff must contact Radio Control to ensure camera coverage of  both the officer and the individual throughout the incident. 

2\. Radio Control must be contacted from the location where the escort begins—not after the individual  has left the property. 

3\. Escorts must always be conducted with at least two officers; no officer is to escort someone alone. 4\. All escorts should proceed to the nearest exit. 

**7\. Banned Patrons** 

A. If a patron who has been issued a ban comes into the transit center while their ban is still valid, Security  staff is to tell that person that they need to leave and that if they return, their ban will be extended.  1\. Security should try to provide a strong physical presence (without going hands on) in front of the  banned individual and turn them around toward the exit.  

2\. Allowing banned individuals to continuously walk back and forth through the Transit Center is not  acceptable.

**8\. Loitering**

A. It is the responsibility of Security staff to break up fights (verbal and physical).  

B. In the event of a physical fight, security shall verbally order individuals to cease behavior.  1\. If unable to deescalate verbally, use only the minimum physical force necessary to cease the fighting  and detain individuals using Vendor-issued handcuffs.  

C. The goal for Security staff is to be proactive versus reactive; do not wait for a progressive argument to get  physical.   
1\. Prior to engaging in any physical altercation, “1128 at ‘Gate \#’” is to be called on the radio.  

**10\. Detainment** 

A. Security will pat down individuals placed in handcuffs for safety purposes only. After the pat down,  Security Staff may ask patrons to empty their pockets if Security detects an object that might be dangerous.  B. Security Staff may ask patrons to empty their pockets or bags if something that appears dangerous is in  plain sight. 

C. Security staff shall provide two on one (2:1) escorts for individuals who are detained in handcuffs.  D. Security Staff are not to leave individuals at any time until properly relieved by another Security Staff or a  Police Officer. 

E. Security staff of the same sex as the detainee must be present whenever possible. 

**11\. Smoking/Vaping** 

A. Security staff shall inform patrons that they must smoke and vape off property (up on the street sidewalk  where the concrete color changes).  

B. Security staff is also to inform patrons they cannot smoke inside the busway, and in the shelters on  Mortimer Street.  

**12\. Bathroom Inspections** 

A. Security staff shall check the male, female, and family restrooms. Bathrooms should be checked a  minimum of two to three times per hour or every 10 minutes collectively. When letting someone in the  family restroom, someone must wait outside the door until the patron exits and then check to make sure the  door secures. 

B. If someone is caught using drugs or alcohol in the bathrooms, we are to announce who we are and ask  them to come out. At this time, we should call for a supervisor and ask for their ID.  

C. Security staff does not have the right or authority to look over bathroom stall doors or walls for any  purpose, including to determine if someone is using drugs/alcohol. If Security staff suspects drugs/alcohol  use is occurring in an enclosed bathroom staff then they shall contact the Police pursuant to established  procedures.

**1\. Overview of Main Street Campus** 

A. Located at 1372 E. Main Street, Rochester, New York 14609

C. Farebox Detail/Service Building 

1\. The Service Building operates seven days a week during the hours of 5 p.m. – 2 a.m. Approximately  200 buses pass through the Service Building every day for refueling, fluids replenishment, farebox cash  removal, and bus washing. 

2\. Security Guards assigned to the farebox detail are required to remove farebox cash boxes and empty  contents directly into farebox vaults, without actually handling cash. This is a physical job in a  production-oriented environment. 

3\. Although working indoors, Security staff are exposed to outside weather conditions, noise and humidity  from the bus wash process. Security staff are expected to work alongside and cooperate with bus  washers under the supervision of a Maintenance Supervisor. 

4\. The fareboxes weigh approximately 17 pounds when empty and can be up to 20 pounds when full. 

**2\. Purpose and Role of Security Staff**

4\. Monitor a camera screen for any suspicious activity for real-time reporting 

5\. Emptying cash fareboxes

**1\. General Duties and Conduct** 

A. Report to duty on time and in a complete, appropriate uniform. All Security staff on this contract are to be  in their assigned area by their scheduled start time.

A. Security staff are prohibited from using the following while on duty: 

1\. Personal cell phones; 

2\. Headphones, iPods, or radios; 

3\. Smartwatches, smart glasses, or Bluetooth devices;

**3\. Detect, Deter and Report**

B. To detect problems, you must be active and alert. 

C. Deterrence is the result of a strong presence of security. Your presence must not just be seen, it must be  seen as active. If you see something suspicious, investigate. The goal is always to be proactive instead of  reactive. 

D. Report all incidents, complaints, responses, and items of interest to RGRTA that occur in and around the  property in your Security Officer Report (SOR) and Incident Reports (IR) when appropriate. B. Security staff will request the individual to leave the campus. 

C. If the individual does not comply, security staff will request additional backup, and will escort the  individual to the exit, informing them they are not permitted to return.
