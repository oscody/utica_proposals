### Technical Proposal Notices

- **A.** No aspect of the Technical Proposal shall reference the pricing.
- **B.** The Technical Proposal shall include:

  1. A Cover Page with Event Name and Number, Vendor name, address, contact information, and date.
  2. Page numbers and a Table of Contents clearly identifying each section.

- **C.** Each section below serves as an Evaluation Criteria and will be scored by RGRTA. Weighting is noted in parenthesis.
