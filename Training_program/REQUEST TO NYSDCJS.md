**REQUEST FOR APPROVAL TO CONDUCT SECURITY GUARD TRAINING THROUGH LIVE VIRTUAL AND TEMPORARY IN-PERSON CLASSROOM FORMAT** 

On behalf of Utica Security Training School, I, Charles Uti, respectfully submit this request for approval to provide New York State security guard training through a live virtual training format. Our goal is to deliver DCJS-required security guard training in a professional, compliant, interactive, and fully supervised virtual classroom environment from our office and an in-person class in a rented per diem environment. 

This request is being made due to an extended delay we are currently experiencing in obtaining FDNY approval since 2023 for our regular office location. To initiate training service, to remain fully compliant and avoid interruption with DCJS requirements, we are requesting permission to conduct training from our office through live virtual instruction and, when needed, from an approved rented classroom location for in-person classes. 

The training will be conducted live by DCJS-certified instructors, not as a self-paced or recorded course. Students will attend in real time, participate actively, ask questions, complete required instructional hours, and meet all attendance and completion requirements before any certificate is issued.

This authorisation request is to provide applicable NYS security guard training courses through live virtual instruction, including:

1. 8-Hour Pre-Assignment Training Course for Security Guards  
2. 16-Hour On-the-Job Training Course for Security Guards  
3. 8-Hour Annual In-Service Training Course for Security Guards

Upon approval of this request, Utica Security Training School’s expected date for the first classroom training session is **July 27, 2026**. The venue has already been booked, and all required equipment, personnel, materials, and accessories necessary to conduct the training are prepared and ready for use. 

**To ensure compliance, accountability, and training integrity, our live virtual training process will include the following safeguards:** 

1)  **Rental Agreement**

Utica Security Training School has entered into a per diem rental arrangement with **DoubleTree by Hilton** for the use of a meeting room as a temporary in-person classroom location. Attached to this request are photographs of the proposed meeting room, documentation showing the square footage of the meeting room, and a letter from hotel management confirming that Utica Security Training School is permitted to rent the meeting room on a per diem basis for training purposes. We respectfully submit these supporting documents for DCJS review and approval of the proposed temporary classroom arrangement. 

2)  **Live Instructor-Led Training**

All classes will be conducted live by a qualified DCJS-certified instructor. The instructor will remain present throughout the entire training session and will monitor student attendance, participation, questions, and completion. The instructor will monitor student engagement throughout the course. Students may be required to answer questions, participate in discussions, complete knowledge checks, and respond when called upon. Lack of participation may result in removal from the class or denial of completion credit. 

3)  **Virtual Platform**

Training will be conducted using a secure live virtual platform, **Zoom Workplace,** for live virtual instruction. The platform allows live video/audio instruction, screen sharing, chat monitoring, attendance tracking, and documentation of student participation. Recordings, attendance reports, chat transcripts, and participation records can be saved and exported for compliance and audit purposes. This platform allows the Division Staff to observe training without a camera.

4)  **Required Instructional Hours and Curriculum**

Utica Security Training will ensure that each course meets the full instructional-hour requirement established by DCJS. Breaks, interruptions, technical delays, and student absences will not be counted toward required training time. All training will follow the applicable DCJS-approved curriculum, lesson objectives, course outlines, and required topics. 

5)  **Administration and Recording Of Testing Standards**

Online 8-hour and OJT testing will be administered through a secure online assessment platform known as **ClassMarker.** Students will complete the exam electronically using their full legal name and email address. The system will record student responses, scores, submission date/time, and pass/fail results. Results will be saved and exported for administrative review, compliance documentation, and audit purposes. Testing will be conducted during the live virtual class session while the instructor monitors students through Zoom.

**Procedure to Ensure 100% Attendance**  
Each student will be required to register with their legal name and provide any valid government government issued photo identification during registration and payment on our **website** before the course begins. Names will automatically be saved on our database for administrative review and access.

Students' identities will be verified at the start of class and may be re-confirmed during the training session by the class instructor. Attendance will be taken at the beginning of class, after breaks, and throughout the training. 

Students will be required to keep their cameras on during the training, except during breaks. Absent students, inactive, logged out, driving, working, or unable to participate will not receive credit. 

**Procedure for Distributing/Completing/Storing Training Forms.**

**Catalogs:** Catalogs will be made available to potential students on the training website. It will consist of courses, course descriptions, and prices.

**Enrolment forms**: Schools will have the enrolment form as the base of registration requirements. Upon registration, students are also enrolled through the documents provided on the training website. 

**Students Advisory Notice:** The training website is configured to automatically send the Advisory Notice to students via email upon completion of payment. Students will be asked to refer to their provided email for registration confirmation, advisory notice, Zoom link, and other relevant information once registration is finalised.

**Procedure For Distributing Signed Certificate Of Completion at Conclusion of Class.**

**Issuance of Certificate:** The certificate will be designed and **locked for editing** using Adobe Pro. Once participating students take and submit the multiple-choice test through ClassMarker, it will be reviewed, and successful students will be emailed their certificate. 

**Upon approval, Utica Security Training has agreed to follow the following guidelines in conducting live virtual New York State Security training:**

* An online training request/announcement with the email subject line “Course Name & Date – Online” will be submitted to the DCJS general inbox at dcjsopssecurityguard@dcjs.ny.gov at least one week before the class. The request will include the course type, training times, instructor name, and a link for DCJS staff to join the class for auditing purposes. Unless a monthly calendar is submitted, each request will be emailed separately.  
* Live virtual training sessions will be recorded and retained for two years. Recordings or access links will be provided to Division staff upon request, in accordance with NYCRR 6028.6(e).  
* At the start of each class, the instructor will complete the DCJS sign-in sheet, identify all students, confirm each student’s ID by video, and record COC status as confirmed on camera.  
* Students must remain visible on camera during the course. Instructors will also remain visible on camera while providing live instruction, including when sharing PowerPoint slides or other presentation materials.  
* Prerecorded content will only be used to supplement live instruction and will not replace live teaching. Such content will not exceed 15 minutes per instructional hour. Previously recorded classes will not be replayed in place of live instruction.
