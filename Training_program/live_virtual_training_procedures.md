# Procedures for Live Virtual Training

**Date Issued:** [not visible in image]  
**Topic:** Procedures for live virtual training

## Offering and Submitting Live Virtual Training

In response to the COVID-19 pandemic and determination security guard training schools are an essential service, the Division of Criminal Justice Services authorized approved security guard training schools to seek approval to provide the Pre-Assignment, OJT, and annual in-service courses in a live virtual format. Due to the positive feedback received from students and schools, the Division has concluded the demand for live virtual training supports the continuation of the initiative indefinitely. As such, this bulletin will outline the procedures schools will follow to request permission to offer live virtual training, instruct in a virtual environment, and report virtual training.

Under the provisions of NYCRR 6027.11(b) and 6028.6(o), the below will be the procedures governing live virtual training.

## Application

To apply for approval to provide training in a live virtual format an approved school shall prepare a written request to provide virtual training and email the request to the general inbox at **dcjsopssecurityguard@dcjs.ny.gov**. The request will include the following:

- Platform school will be using (Zoom, WebEx, Teams, etc.).
  - Have ability to administer and record required Pre and OJT test.
  - Allow recording of virtual session.
  - Allow Division staff to observe training without a camera required.
- Procedure school will employ to ensure 100% attendance.
- Procedure for distributing/completing/storing school forms — enrollment form, catalog and student advisory notice.
- Procedure for distributing signed certificate of completion at conclusion of class.
  - Certificate must be locked for editing.
  - This will require Adobe Pro.

## Conducting Virtual Training

Upon receiving approval, the following shall be the guidelines for conducting live virtual training:

- Online training request/announcement emailed to the DCJS general inbox **dcjsopssecurityguard@dcjs.ny.gov** at least one week in advance.
  - Request must contain type of course, times, instructor, and link for DCJS to follow to join the class for auditing purposes.
  - Subject of request email should be “course name & date — online”.
    - i.e. Pre Assignment 12/15/22 — online
  - Unless submitting a monthly calendar, requests shall be emailed separately.
- Live virtual training must be recorded and retained for two years.
  - Recording, or link, must be shared with Division staff upon request per NYCRR 6028.6(e).
- At start of class complete the DCJS sign-in sheet identifying all students and entering COC (confirmed on camera).
  - Instructor must confirm students’ ID via video at start of class.
- Students must be visible on camera during the course.
  - Students may turn cameras off during breaks.
- Instructor must be visible on camera when providing live instruction.
  - This includes when sharing a PowerPoint.
- Prerecorded content may only be used to augment live instruction and not replace live instruction.
  - Use of video shall not exceed 15 minutes per instructional hour.
- Replaying a previously recorded class in lieu of live instruction is prohibited and can lead to disciplinary action against the school and instructor.

## Submitting Live Virtual Training

When entering completed virtual training in the Acadis Portal, the school must note **“online training”** after the instructor’s name in the Instruction Provider section and in the comment section. Failure to provide this information in either section will result in rejection of the roster.

Sign in sheet shall be completed in accordance with Training Bulletin 699-1-22 which stipulates the instructor is responsible for confirming identity of all participants at the start of the virtual training, then entering COC (Confirmed on Camera) in the signature field for the student. The sign in sheet shall then be attached using the Acadis Documents feature as outlined in the bulletin.
