## Missing Or Needs To Be Added

- [ ] Add DCJS approval/request workflow before training can start.
  - Requirement: Email a written request to `dcjsopssecurityguard@dcjs.ny.gov`.
  - The request must include platform, attendance procedure, school forms procedure, and certificate procedure.
  - Procedure proof: `live_virtual_training_procedures.md` lines 14-24.

- [ ] Add one-week advance class announcement workflow.
  - Requirement: Email online training request/announcement to DCJS at least one week before class.
  - Include course type, times, instructor, and link for DCJS audit access.
  - Procedure proof: `live_virtual_training_procedures.md` lines 30-34.

- [ ] Add DCJS audit access requirement.
  - Requirement: Platform must allow Division staff to observe training without requiring their camera.
  - Procedure proof: `live_virtual_training_procedures.md` line 19.

- [ ] Add DCJS sign-in sheet process.
  - Requirement: At the start of class, complete the DCJS sign-in sheet and mark students as `COC`.
  - Procedure proof: `live_virtual_training_procedures.md` lines 37-38 and 51.

- [ ] Add student ID verification by video.
  - Requirement: Instructor must confirm each student's ID by video at the start of class.
  - Procedure proof: `live_virtual_training_procedures.md` lines 37-38 and 51.

- [ ] Add form distribution, completion, and storage process.
  - Requirement: Define how enrollment forms, catalog, and student advisory notice will be distributed, completed, and stored.
  - Procedure proof: `live_virtual_training_procedures.md` line 21.

- [ ] Add locked certificate requirement.
  - Requirement: Signed certificates must be locked for editing.
  - Note: Procedure says this requires Adobe Pro.
  - Procedure proof: `live_virtual_training_procedures.md` lines 22-24.

- [ ] Add recording-share process for Division staff requests.
  - Requirement: Recording or recording link must be shared with Division staff upon request.
  - Procedure proof: `live_virtual_training_procedures.md` lines 35-36.

- [ ] Add prerecorded video time limit.
  - Requirement: Prerecorded content may only augment live instruction and may not exceed 15 minutes per instructional hour.
  - Procedure proof: `live_virtual_training_procedures.md` lines 43-44.

- [ ] Add instructor camera visibility rule.
  - Requirement: Instructor must be visible on camera during live instruction, including while sharing PowerPoint.
  - Procedure proof: `live_virtual_training_procedures.md` lines 41-42.

- [ ] Add Acadis Portal reporting process.
  - Requirement: Enter `online training` after the instructor's name and in the comment section.
  - Risk: Missing this can cause roster rejection.
  - Procedure proof: `live_virtual_training_procedures.md` lines 47-51.

## Recommended MVP Update
